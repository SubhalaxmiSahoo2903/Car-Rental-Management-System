ABOUT THE PROJECT

Car Rental Management System

A simple and efficient system for managing cars, customers and rental transactions.

PROJECT INTRODUCTION

Making Car Rental Simple & Efficient

The Car Rental and Booking Management System is designed to manage the complete car rental process in an organized manner.

The system maintains information about cars, customers and rental transactions. It allows users to search available cars, register customers, rent cars, return cars and calculate rental charges.

The system also maintains rental history and automatically updates the availability status of cars after renting and returning.

PROJECT OBJECTIVE

What Does The System Do?

🚗

Manage Cars

Add, remove and maintain car information including registration number, brand, model, type and rental rate.

👤

Manage Customers

Register customers and maintain their customer ID, name, contact number and driving license information.

📅

Manage Rentals

Rent cars, return cars and calculate rental charges according to the rental duration.

📋

Rental History

Maintain records of previous and active rental transactions for easy reference.

SYSTEM FEATURES

Main Features

01

Add and Remove Cars

The system allows administrators to add new cars and remove existing cars.

02

Register Customers

Customer information can be registered and maintained in the system.

03

Search Available Cars

Users can search for cars based on availability and other car details.

04

Rent a Car

A customer can rent an available car by selecting the required rental period.

05

Calculate Rental Charges

Rental charges are calculated using the rental rate and number of days.

06

Return a Car

After returning a vehicle, its availability status is updated.

07

Update Availability

A rented car becomes unavailable and becomes available again after return.

08

Display Rental History

The system maintains and displays rental transaction records.
